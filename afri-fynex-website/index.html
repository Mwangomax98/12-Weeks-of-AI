About Us

Afri Fynex Solutions Limited is a Tanzanian technology-based company creating practical digital products that help people and businesses thrive in an increasingly digital world.

We focus on the everyday: keeping business records in order, getting from place to place and making payments. Our goal is to make these simpler, better connected and more convenient for individuals, entrepreneurs, small and medium-sized businesses, drivers, merchants and growing enterprises.

We believe digital tools should solve real problems, be easy to use and create meaningful value for the people who rely on them.

Vision, Mission & Purpose

Our Vision

To pioneer innovative digital solutions that empower African businesses to thrive in global markets.

Our Mission

To transform lives and empower businesses through innovative, accessible, and impactful digital technologies.

Our Purpose

To make technology work better for people and businesses. We turn everyday challenges into opportunities through smart, accessible, and human-centred digital solutions that simplify life, unlock potential, and accelerate sustainable growth.

Our Promise

We deliver technology that is intuitive, intelligent, secure by design, and built around real-world needs. Through continuous innovation, responsible development, and a commitment to our users, we create digital experiences that inspire trust, deliver value, and move businesses forward.

Our Founders

HK

Hendry Kanje

Co-Founder

Hendry Kanje is an entrepreneur, author and brand builder with a passion for turning ideas into practical solutions. He founded Trophacom, a home-appliance brand offering gas cookers, blenders, ceiling fans, irons, solar lights and rechargeable lighting, and TAI, a brand of genuine mobile-phone accessories including chargers, earbuds, flash drives and memory cards.

He is the author of three books: Demokrasia ya Vurugu, Wealth Hub and National Security: Who Should Be Responsible? As a Kariakoo business leader and spokesman, he works closely with the challenges and opportunities facing local entrepreneurs. Through his work, he champions innovation, entrepreneurship and the power of ideas to change everyday lives.
